My Travel Experiences

In Japan, there is a saying called Ichigo Ichie, which means "one time, one meeting." It refers to the idea of treasuring each encounter and moment, as they may never happen again.

Travel is the perfect example of Ichigo Ichie. Every journey is filled with unique experiences that can only be found in that particular place and at that particular time. The beauty of nature, culture, history, architecture, food, atmosphere, climate, and the people all contribute to the charm of a destination. I believe that travel is valuable because it allows us to experience these once-in-a-lifetime moments that cannot be fully understood through knowledge or photographs alone. At the same time, I also enjoy capturing those special moments with my camera.

Over the years, I have visited many countries, and each one has been filled with its own unique beauty and character.
